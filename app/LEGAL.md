## Privacy

### What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following on a private server I manage:

- **Your account:** your name, email address, and login, through the website's account system (WordPress).
- **Your practice:** your declarations (including earlier wordings as you refine one), the practices you log, the moods you choose, your morning clearings, gratitude and rest entries, notes, and weekly reflection answers.
- **Your progress:** streaks, streak freezes, practice counts, and your timezone.

### Who can see it

- **You.** Your entries are visible only to you in the app.
- **Me, in a limited way.** I can see usage counts (for example, how many days someone practiced) to keep the app working and improve it. I don’t read your entries. Because I run the server, the database is technically accessible to me as its administrator. That’s true of most apps, whether or not their creators say so explicitly; I’m telling you so you can decide what to write.
- **No one else.** I don't and won't sell, rent, share your information, or use it for advertising without your explicit consent.
- **About AI:** I use AI tools to help write and review the app’s code. They work only with the code, which is public, and with made-up test data, never with your account or entries. Your entries live only in the app’s database on my server, which isn’t part of the public code and isn’t shared with AI tools or anyone reviewing the code.
- [FUTURE: If friends or community sharing are added, only what you choose to share will be visible to others. If end-to-end encryption is added, then even the database administrator wouldn't be able to access your saved content. If an optional AI feature is added, it will be off unless you turn it on, and this page will explain exactly what it sends and where.]

### Voice input

If you use the microphone button, your browser's built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text. Depending on your browser, that audio is processed by **Google** (Chrome, Edge) or **Apple** (Safari) under their privacy policies, not sent to my server. Only the text you choose to save reaches the app. Typing is always an option.

### What the app doesn't do

- No third-party analytics or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, no ad pixels).
- No cookies beyond the ones needed to keep you logged in.
- No AI processing of your entries. [FUTURE: If an optional AI pattern-review feature is added, it will be off unless you turn it on, and this page will say which service it uses.]

### Where it's stored and for how long

Your data is stored on a server hosted by DigitalOcean in the United States. It's kept until you ask me to delete it, or until the app is shut down. Server backups may keep copies for up to 14 days after deletion.

### Your choices

- **Get a copy** of your entries: contact me using the contact information below. [FUTURE: an export button in the app.]
- **Delete your data:** email me and I'll delete your account and everything you've written within [28] days.
- **Correct your information:** edit your account in the website's profile settings, or email me.
